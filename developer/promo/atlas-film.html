The Glassmakers of Venice

CBy the thirteenth century, Venetian glass was prized across Europe, and the city guarded its secrets closely. Craftsmen were moved to the island of Murano, partly to contain the risk of fire. Even so, some glassmakers were allowed to travel abroad, provided they returned. Those who left without permission faced severe penalties.
